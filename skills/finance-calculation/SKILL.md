# Finance calculation skill

When this skill is loaded:

1. Identify the two periods being compared.
2. Use the available revenue-growth tool for arithmetic instead of estimating.
3. Show the formula in plain language.
4. Report money in millions of US dollars and round to one decimal place.
5. Say when the requested periods are not in the dataset.
